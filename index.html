Academy for Pharmaceutical Research and Healthcare

The Academy for Pharmaceutical Research and Healthcare (APRH) is an independent professional academic society dedicated to advancing pharmaceutical sciences, healthcare research, and interdisciplinary collaboration. APRH serves as a global platform that connects researchers, academicians, healthcare professionals, clinicians, industry experts, and students with the common objective of promoting high-quality research and innovation.

Our academy is committed to fostering excellence in scientific research, encouraging evidence-based healthcare practices, and supporting the dissemination of knowledge that ultimately improves patient care and public health. As pharmaceutical and healthcare sciences continue to evolve rapidly, APRH aims to create opportunities for networking, collaborative research, academic development, and professional growth through scientific activities, educational initiatives, and scholarly publications.

Our Vision

To become a globally recognized academic society that promotes excellence in pharmaceutical research, healthcare innovation, education, and scientific collaboration for the betterment of society.

Core Focus Areas

  • Excellence: Advancing high standards in pharmaceutical research and scientific work.

  • Innovation: Improving healthcare and finding new ways to help patients.

  • Education: Sharing knowledge and training future professionals.

  • Collaboration: Working together across the globe for social good.

Our Mission

Advancing the frontiers of pharmaceutical sciences and global healthcare.

  • Promote high-quality pharmaceutical and healthcare research.

  • Encourage multidisciplinary collaboration among researchers worldwide.

  • Support students and early-career researchers through mentoring and educational opportunities.

  • Facilitate scientific exchange through conferences, workshops, webinars, and training programs.

  • Promote ethical research practices and scientific integrity.

  • Encourage publication of quality research in reputable scientific journals.

  • Bridge academia, healthcare institutions, regulatory agencies, and the pharmaceutical industry.

Core Objectives

  • Build a strong international network of pharmaceutical and healthcare professionals.

  • Promote collaborative research projects across institutions and countries.

  • Organize scientific meetings, seminars, workshops, and conferences.

  • Support continuous professional development and lifelong learning.

  • Encourage innovation in pharmaceutical sciences and healthcare technologies.

  • Provide educational resources for researchers and students.

  • Recognize excellence through awards and academic honors.

  • Facilitate partnerships with universities, research institutes, healthcare organizations, and industry.

Your Questions and Answers

Find quick answers about APRH, its membership, activities, journal, and contact details.

APRH is an independent professional academic society dedicated to advancing pharmaceutical sciences, healthcare research, and interdisciplinary collaboration. It serves as a global platform connecting researchers, academicians, healthcare professionals, clinicians, industry experts, and students to promote high-quality research and innovation.

Membership is open to pharmaceutical researchers, faculty members, scientists, healthcare professionals, pharmacists, physicians, nurses, biotechnology professionals, pharmaceutical industry professionals, regulatory professionals, postgraduate students, and undergraduate students interested in research.

APRH organizes scientific meetings, seminars, workshops, conferences, webinars, training programs, educational initiatives, professional development activities, and collaborative research opportunities. It also supports awards, honors, and knowledge-sharing programs.

The official journal is the Asian Journal of Pharmaceutical Research and Healthcare (AJPRHC), published by Wolters Kluwer – Medknow. It is a peer-reviewed international journal covering pharmacology, clinical pharmacy, pharmaceutics, pharmaceutical chemistry, pharmacognosy, pharmacovigilance, regulatory affairs, clinical research, drug discovery, biotechnology, public health, hospital pharmacy, healthcare management, precision medicine, drug safety, toxicology, and translational research.
