Official Journal


Academy for Pharmaceutical Research and Healthcare

Asian Journal of Pharmaceutical Research and Healthcare (AJPRHC) is the official scholarly journal of the Academy for Pharmaceutical Research and Healthcare (APRH), published by Wolters Kluwer – Medknow.

AJPRHC

Peer-reviewed international journal dedicated to publishing high-quality research in pharmaceutical sciences, clinical pharmacy, pharmacology, healthcare, and related biomedical disciplines.

Why Publish with AJPRHC?

  • Rigorous peer-review process
  • International editorial expertise
  • Global readership
  • High publication standards
  • Ethical publication practices
  • Online accessibility
  • Rapid dissemination of scientific knowledge

Scope of the Journal

  • Pharmacology
  • Clinical Pharmacy
  • Pharmaceutics
  • Pharmaceutical Chemistry
  • Pharmacognosy
  • Pharmacovigilance
  • Regulatory Affairs
  • Clinical Research
  • Drug Discovery and Development
  • Pharmaceutical Biotechnology
  • Public Health
  • Hospital Pharmacy
  • Healthcare Management
  • Precision Medicine
  • Drug Safety
  • Toxicology
  • Translational Research

International Platform

The journal provides a global platform for researchers, clinicians, academicians, healthcare professionals, and students to disseminate original scientific findings and evidence-based research.

For journal information, author guidelines, current issues, and manuscript submission, please visit the official journal website: ajprhc.com

Ethical

Committed to ethical publication practices, high standards, and responsible scientific communication.
